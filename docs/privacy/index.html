Privacy Policy – Azulejo Mahjong

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Azulejo Mahjong (“the app”) is a tile-matching solitaire game for Android made by Damian, an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This page explains what information the app handles and your choices. If you have any question, write to damian@d2600.com.

In short

  • There is no account or sign-in. I don’t ask for your name, email, contacts or photos.
  • Your game (progress, settings and the game in progress) is saved only on your phone. I don’t receive it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects some information from your device, such as its advertising ID and IP address.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.

1. Information saved on your phone

The app saves these things on your phone so you can keep playing where you left off:

  • Your settings: tile size, high contrast and language.
  • Your progress: levels won, daily challenges completed and streaks.
  • The game you are playing.
  • How many boards you have won since the last ad, and how many free hints you have used today.
  • Whether you bought “Remove ads”.

This information is not sent to me or to anyone else by the app. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. If you have turned on Android’s backup, Android may include it in your device backup in your Google account; that backup is managed by Google, not by me.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows two kinds of ads, both from Google AdMob:

  • A full-screen ad after some completed boards (never in the middle of a game).
  • Optional videos you can choose to watch to get an extra hint or shuffle.

To show these ads, prevent fraud and measure how they perform, Google’s advertising software in the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

InformationWhat it is
IP addressYour device’s internet address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not your exact position).
Device identifiersAndroid’s advertising ID and app set ID.
Interactions with adsFor example, app launches, taps and video views.
Diagnostic informationPerformance data, such as loading time and crashes.

This information is encrypted when it is sent. Google uses it according to its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before Google can use your information for personalized ads.
  • If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your information for personalized ads.
  • Where these laws apply, you can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.
  • On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone’s settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • If you buy “Remove ads”, the full-screen ads stop. The optional videos stay available only if you choose to watch them.

3. Purchases

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; I don’t receive your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns “Remove ads”, so it can turn the ads off and restore the purchase on a new phone.

4. What I don’t do

  • I don’t collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • I don’t use analytics or tracking tools of my own, and I don’t run servers that store your data.
  • I don’t sell your personal information for money. Showing personalized ads through Google may count as “selling” or “sharing” under some US state laws; see “Your choices about ads” above to opt out.

5. Children

The app is designed for adults and is not directed to children under 13. I don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want help, contact me at the address below.

6. Keeping and deleting information

Everything the app saves stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. I don’t hold a copy. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google’s policies; resetting or deleting your advertising ID stops it from being linked to your previous ID.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.
